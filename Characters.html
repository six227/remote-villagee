角色背景
A spirit of Unfortunate Bridge. On October 8, 1955, a bus carrying passengers crashed off a bridge in Sai Kung, killing all 32 people on board. A Ming was the youngest victim, only seven years old. He lost his harmonica, which he was going to play for his grandmother.
