玩法說明 Gameplay

🎭

多重結局 Multiple Endings

在遊戲中，你的每一個關鍵選擇都會導向不同的命運。歸還遺物，亡魂得以解脫，你也將獲得祝福；若選擇佔為己有，則可能永遠被困在迷霧之中。
Every critical choice you make leads to a different fate. Return the lost items, and the spirits will find peace, blessing you in return. But if you choose to keep them for yourself, you may become trapped in the mist forever.
🔍

線索收集 Clue Collection

在鎖羅盆村、新娘潭、猛鬼橋等場景中，隱藏著亡魂們遺失的物品。收集這些線索，不僅能拼湊出意外的真相，更能幫助亡魂解脫。
Hidden across locations like So Lo Pun Village, Bride's Pool, and Unfortunate Bridge are the lost belongings of lingering spirits. Collecting these clues not only helps piece together the truth but also allows the spirits to find peace.
🖱️
