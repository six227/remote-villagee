玩法說明 Gameplay

線索收集 Clue Collection

🔍
在鎖羅盆村、新娘潭、猛鬼橋等場景中，隱藏著亡魂們遺失的物品。收集這些線索，不僅能拼湊出意外的真相，更能幫助亡魂解脫。
Hidden across locations like So Lo Pun Village, Bride's Pool, and Unfortunate Bridge are the lost belongings of lingering spirits. Collecting these clues not only helps piece together the truth but also allows the spirits to find peace.
📖
每一件遺物都承載著一段未完成的故事。找到它們，歸還給正確的人，你將改變他們的命運。
Each lost item carries an unfinished story. Find them, return them to the right person, and you may change their fate.
✨
梳子、繡花鞋、首飾盒、口琴、書包、圍巾⋯⋯每一件遺物，都是解開謎團的關鍵。
Combs, embroidered shoes, jewelry boxes, harmonicas, schoolbags, scarves... each lost item is the key to unraveling the mystery.

新娘潭結局 Bride's Pool Endings

在新娘潭，玩家將會遇到哭泣的新娘阿鳳。途中會發現她的遺物——梳子、繡花鞋、首飾盒。玩家的選擇：是否撿起？是否歸還？將導向截然不同的命運。

At Bride's Pool, players will encounter the weeping bride A Feng. Along the way, they will discover her lost belongings — a comb, embroidered shoes, and a jewelry box. The player's choices — whether to pick them up and whether to return them — will lead to completely different fates.

< img id="endingImg" src="images/bride_good.jpg" alt="新娘潭結局">
好結局：歸還遺物
如果玩家撿起部分或全部遺物，並歸還給新娘阿鳳，她將與等待已久的新郎重逢。主角會獲得新娘的頭紗（祝福道具），阿鳳與新郎走向光芒，水面泛起溫柔的波光。
Good Ending: Return the Belongings
If the player picks up some or all of the lost items and returns them to the bride A Feng, she will reunite with her long-waiting groom. The protagonist receives the bride's veil (a blessed item), as A Feng and her groom walk into the light, gentle ripples spreading across the water.
