玩法說明 Gameplay

新娘潭結局 Bride's Pool Endings

在新娘潭，玩家將會遇到哭泣的新娘阿鳳。途中會發現她的遺物——梳子、繡花鞋、首飾盒。玩家的選擇：是否撿起？是否歸還？將導向截然不同的命運。

At Bride's Pool, players will encounter the weeping bride A Feng. Along the way, they will discover her lost belongings — a comb, embroidered shoes, and a jewelry box. The player's choices — whether to pick them up and whether to return them — will lead to completely different fates.

< img src="images/bride_good.jpg" alt="好結局">
好結局：歸還遺物
如果玩家撿起部分或全部遺物，並歸還給新娘阿鳳，她將與等待已久的新郎重逢。主角會獲得新娘的頭紗（祝福道具），阿鳳與新郎走向光芒，水面泛起溫柔的波光。
Good Ending: Return the Belongings
If the player picks up some or all of the lost items and returns them to the bride A Feng, she will reunite with her long-waiting groom. The protagonist receives the bride's veil (a blessed item), as A Feng and her groom walk into the light, gentle ripples spreading across the water.
< img src="images/bride_bad.jpg" alt="壞結局">
壞結局：新娘的邀請
如果玩家企圖佔有遺物，說謊說沒有拿到，阿鳳會憤怒地將主角拖入潭底。無數蒼白的手抓住主角，永遠被困在新娘潭，解鎖「新娘的邀請」結局。
Bad Ending: The Bride's Invitation
If the player tries to keep the belongings and lies about not having them, A Feng will angrily drag the protagonist into the pool. Countless pale hands grab the protagonist, trapping them forever beneath Bride's Pool, unlocking the 'Bride's Invitation' ending.
< img src="images/bride_neutral.jpg" alt="中立路線">
中立路線：苦苦哀求
如果玩家一樣東西都沒有拿，阿鳳不會生氣，只是哀傷地請求主角幫忙去拿遺物。主角答應後，去取回梳子、繡花鞋和首飾盒，後續可走向好結局。
Neutral Path: A Pleading Cry
If the player takes nothing at all, A Feng does not get angry. Instead, she sorrowfully begs the protagonist to help retrieve her lost belongings. The protagonist agrees, retrieves the comb, embroidered shoes, and jewelry box, and can still reach the good ending.

猛鬼橋結局 Unfortunate Bridge Endings

在猛鬼橋，玩家將會遇到一九五五年巴士墜橋意外中罹難的亡魂。途中會發現三件遺物——阿明的口琴、王小玲的書包、母親的圍巾。玩家的選擇：是否撿起？是否歸還？將導向截然不同的命運。

At Unfortunate Bridge, players will encounter the spirits of the 1955 bus crash. Along the way, they will discover three lost items — A Ming's harmonica, Wang Xiaoling's schoolbag, and her mother's scarf. The player's choices — whether to pick them up and whether to return them — will lead to completely different fates.

< img src="images/bridge_good.jpg" alt="好結局">
好結局：歸還遺物
如果玩家撿起遺物並歸還，阿明重新吹起《月光光》，王小玲與母親重逢，三十二位亡魂一起走向光芒。主角獲得發光的車票和一小截藍色毛線。
Good Ending: Return the Belongings
If the player picks up and returns the lost items, A Ming plays "Moonlight" again, Wang Xiaoling reunites with her mother, and all 32 spirits walk into the light together. The protagonist receives a glowing ticket and a small piece of blue yarn.
< img src="images/bridge_bad.jpg" alt="壞結局">
壞結局：永遠被困
如果玩家企圖佔有遺物，亡魂們會憤怒地將主角困在猛鬼橋下，永遠無法離開，成為第33個亡魂。
Bad Ending: Trapped Forever
If the player tries to keep the belongings, the spirits will angrily trap the protagonist beneath Unfortunate Bridge, never to leave, becoming the 33rd spirit.
< img src="images/bridge_neutral.jpg" alt="中立路線">
中立路線：答應幫忙
如果玩家沒有拿任何遺物，亡魂們會哀傷地請求主角幫忙。主角答應後，取回遺物歸還，最終走向好結局。
Neutral Path: Promise to Help
If the player takes nothing at all, the spirits sorrowfully beg the protagonist for help. The protagonist agrees, retrieves the items, returns them, and ultimately reaches the good ending.

線索收集 Clue Collection

< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_comb.jpg" alt="梳子">
梳子
新娘阿鳳的梳子。母親在她出嫁前為她梳頭：「一梳梳到尾，二梳白髮齊眉，三梳子孫滿堂。」
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_shoe.jpg" alt="繡花鞋">
繡花鞋
新娘阿鳳的繡花鞋。她親手繡了三個月，穿上腳的時候很開心，落水時從腳上脫落。
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_box.jpg" alt="首飾盒">
首飾盒
新娘阿鳳的首飾盒。裡面本來有一隻玉鐲，是丈夫承諾過門之後親手為她戴上的信物。
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_harmonica.jpg" alt="口琴">
口琴
阿明的口琴。七歲的他準備吹《月光光》給外婆聽，巴士墜橋時口琴從手中飛出。
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_bag.jpg" alt="書包">
書包
王小玲的書包。裡面裝著作業本和一隻毛絨玩具，作業本最後一頁寫著：「我好開心。」
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_scarf.jpg" alt="圍巾">
圍巾
王小玲母親的圍巾。她怕女兒著涼，用圍巾蓋在女兒身上，風吹走圍巾，她伸手抓不到。
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_veil.jpg" alt="新娘的頭紗">
新娘的頭紗
阿鳳留下的祝福。歸還遺物後，水流將頭紗推到岸邊，阿替說：「這是她的祝福，收下吧。」
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_ticket.jpg" alt="發光的車票">
發光的車票
猛鬼橋亡魂留下的祝福。歸還遺物後，主角手中多了一片發光的車票，象徵他們終於可以離開。
< img class="clue-card-img" src="images/clue_yarn.jpg" alt="一小截藍色毛線">
一小截藍色毛線
王小玲母親圍巾上的一小截毛線。她和小玲一起走向光芒時，留下了這份溫暖的記憶。
